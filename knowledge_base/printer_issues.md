# Printer Setup and Troubleshooting
Category: Hardware and Peripherals

## Overview
Office printers are named by floor and number, for example PRN-3F-02 is printer 2 on the third floor. All printers require a connection to the Acme-Corp network.

## Adding a Printer
Open Settings, go to Printers and scanners, click Add device, and select the printer by its name. If it is not listed, click The printer I want is not listed and enter the name shown on the printer label.

## Print Job Stuck in the Queue
Open Printers and scanners, select the printer, and open the print queue. Cancel all documents. If the queue will not clear, open the Services app, find Print Spooler, and choose Restart. Then print again.

## Printer Shows Offline
Check that the laptop is on the Acme-Corp network. Remove the printer and add it again. Confirm that the printer screen is on and shows no error.

## Paper Jams and Toner
For a paper jam, follow the instructions on the printer screen. To request toner or report a hardware fault, create a helpdesk request that includes the printer name and the error shown.

## Escalation
If a printer is offline for everyone on the floor, create a ticket for Desktop Support and include the printer name.
