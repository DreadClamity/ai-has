# Password Reset Procedure
Category: Account Access

## Overview
This procedure covers the Windows domain account, company email, and the intranet, which all use the same password. Passwords expire every 90 days, and users receive an email reminder 14 days before expiry.

## Self-Service Reset (Preferred Method)
1. Open https://selfservice.acme.example/reset in a browser.
2. Enter your employee email address.
3. Approve the verification request in your authenticator app, or type the 6-digit code shown there.
4. Enter a new password twice and click Submit.
5. Wait 5 minutes, then sign in again on all devices.

## Password Requirements
The new password must be at least 12 characters long and include an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number, and a symbol. It cannot match any of your last 5 passwords and cannot contain your name or employee ID.

## Account Lockout
After 5 failed sign-in attempts the account locks for 30 minutes and unlocks automatically. To unlock sooner, use the self-service reset page above or contact the IT Service Desk.

## When Self-Service Fails
If you have no access to your authenticator app, contact the IT Service Desk. An agent will verify your identity using your employee ID and your manager's name, then issue a temporary password that is valid for 24 hours. You must change the temporary password at your next sign-in. Never share your password or temporary password in chat or email.

## Escalation
Escalate to the Security team if the account stays locked after 30 minutes, or if the user suspects someone else accessed the account.
