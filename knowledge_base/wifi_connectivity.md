# Wi-Fi Connectivity Troubleshooting
Category: Network and Remote Access

## Overview
Acme Corp has two wireless networks. Acme-Corp is for employees and uses your domain email and password. Acme-Guest is for visitors, and reception gives out a new access code every day.

## Cannot Connect to Acme-Corp
1. Turn Wi-Fi off and on again, and make sure Airplane mode is off.
2. Open Wi-Fi settings, select Acme-Corp, choose Forget, then reconnect.
3. Enter your domain email and your current password. If you changed your password recently, use the new one.
4. Restart the laptop and try again.

## Connected But No Internet
Disconnect and reconnect to the network. Open a browser and try a public website. If a sign-in page appears, complete it. Restart the laptop if the problem continues.

## Slow or Dropping Connection
Move closer to an access point, avoid crowded areas such as the cafeteria at lunch, and close heavy downloads. Check whether coworkers nearby have the same issue.

## Escalation
If several people in the same area lose Wi-Fi at once, create a ticket for the Network team with the floor, the location, and the time it started.
